About Us


Kwawala Energies is dedicated to transforming lives by providing access to affordable and sustainable solar energy solutions. With a mission to empower underprivileged households in Malawi, Kwawala Energies focuses on promoting economic empowerment, enhancing livelihood resilience, fostering environmental sustainability, and advancing education through innovative solar technologies.

Approach

Kwawala Energies adopts a market-driven approach, leveraging Village Savings and Loans (VSL) groups to distribute solar products. This model ensures financial inclusion and affordability, allowing communities to access solar energy through flexible payment plans.

Sign-up to our latest updates

Key Initiatives
  • Baseline Surveys: Conducting research to assess community needs and develop strategic interventions.
  • Community Engagement: Collaborating with local leaders and VSL groups to ensure widespread adoption.
  • Product Distribution: Offering a range of solar home systems, including SunKing Pico, Home 40, and Home 200.
  • Financial Flexibility: Implementing credit models that allow households to pay for solar products in manageable installments.
  • Monitoring & Evaluation: Conducting routine follow-ups to assess impact, provide technical support, and ensure sustainability.

Activities

Kwawala Energies is actively engaged in the sale of diverse solar lamp products, including the SunKing Home 200x, Home 40 Plus, and Pico Solar Lamps, in Mulanje District. This initiative addresses the critical energy needs of communities affected by climate events like Cyclone Freddy in 2023. The project, supported by D-Prize funding, aligns with the organization’s goal to improve energy access in underserved regions.
