About Us


Kwawala Energies is dedicated to transforming lives by providing access to affordable and sustainable solar energy solutions. With a mission to empower underprivileged households in Malawi, Kwawala Energies focuses on promoting economic empowerment, enhancing livelihood resilience, fostering environmental sustainability, and advancing education through innovative solar technologies.

Our Approach

Kwawala Energies adopts a market-driven approach, leveraging Village Savings and Loans (VSL) groups to distribute solar products.

Key Initiatives
  • Baseline Surveys
  • Community Engagement
  • Product Distribution
  • Financial Flexibility
  • Monitoring & Evaluation

Activities

Kwawala Energies is actively engaged in the sale of diverse solar lamp products, including the SunKing Home 200x, Home 40 Plus, and Pico Solar Lamps, in Mulanje District. This initiative addresses the critical energy needs of communities affected by climate events like Cyclone Freddy in 2023. The project, supported by D-Prize funding, aligns with the organization’s goal to improve energy access in underserved regions.
